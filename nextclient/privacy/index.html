Privacy Policy

NextClient is a local-first Android follow-up assistant published by BOTIRJON R. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and the controls available to you.

Effective date: October 5, 2026
Support: huawey1222@gmail.com

Information stored on your device

NextClient stores information you enter, including client names, company details, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, potential deal values, interaction history, follow-ups and preferences. This data is stored in the app's private local storage. No NextClient account is required, and the current app has no developer-operated cloud sync or cloud backup for these records.

The app also keeps bounded local usage counters for product validation. These counters use fixed categories, do not contain client content, and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Backups and deletion

Export and restore use Android's document picker. A backup can contain client information and is not encrypted by NextClient; you choose where it is saved. A third-party storage provider may store or sync that file under its own policy. Deleting app data does not delete copies you exported elsewhere.

Settings includes a confirmed “Delete all local data” action that clears local business records, preferences, local usage counters and scheduled reminders. Because the current app has no NextClient account or server-side copy of client records, the developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase information stored only on your device.

Notifications

NextClient can schedule local follow-up reminders. Notification permission is requested only when needed. Reminder text may appear on your lock screen depending on Android settings. You can disable notifications in Android settings; core app functionality remains available.

Voice input and external contact apps

Optional voice entry opens a speech-recognition service available on your device. That provider may process audio remotely under its own terms. NextClient does not request microphone permission or store audio recordings; returned text is saved only if you choose to save it.

Call, email and messaging actions open external apps. Those providers receive the destination or content you choose to send and operate under their own privacy policies. NextClient does not read your address book and does not assume an external contact attempt succeeded.

Google Play Billing and platform services

The app includes Google Play Billing infrastructure. Public paid checkout is disabled in the current validation configuration. Explicit Pro or restore actions may communicate with Google Play. Google and related SDKs may process service diagnostics, technical identifiers and purchase-related information under their applicable policies. NextClient does not send client records to Google Play Billing.

Android and Google Play may collect crash, ANR and platform diagnostics according to platform settings and policies. NextClient currently includes no third-party advertising SDK and no developer-operated remote analytics service.

Security

NextClient uses Android app-private storage, disables Android automatic app backup for business data, and disallows cleartext traffic for app-owned networking. These controls reduce exposure but cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep exported backup files in a location you trust.

Children

NextClient is a business productivity app and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes

This policy will be updated before introducing materially different data handling, such as developer-operated cloud sync, remote analytics, account services or server-side purchase verification.
