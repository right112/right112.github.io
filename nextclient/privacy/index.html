Privacy Policy

NextClient is a local-first Android follow-up assistant published by BOTIRJON R. This policy explains what the app stores, what may leave your device, and the controls available to you.

Effective date: October 7, 2026
Support: huawey1222@gmail.com

Information stored on your device

NextClient stores information you enter, including client names, company details, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, potential deal values, interaction history, follow-ups and preferences. This data is stored in app-private local storage. No NextClient account is required, and NextClient does not provide developer-operated cloud sync or cloud backup for client records.

The app may keep bounded local usage counters for product functionality. These counters do not contain client content and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Backups and deletion

Export and restore use Android's document picker. A backup can contain client information and is not encrypted by NextClient; you choose where it is saved. A third-party storage provider may store or sync that file under its own policy. Deleting app data does not delete copies you exported elsewhere.

Settings includes a confirmed “Delete all local data” action that clears local business records, preferences, local usage counters and scheduled reminders. Because NextClient has no account or server-side copy of client records, the developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase information stored only on your device.

Notifications

NextClient can schedule local follow-up reminders. Notification permission is requested only when needed. Reminder text may appear on your lock screen depending on Android settings. You can disable notifications in Android settings; core app functionality remains available.

Voice input and external contact apps

Optional voice entry opens a speech-recognition service available on your device. That provider may process audio remotely under its own terms. NextClient does not request microphone permission or store audio recordings; returned text is saved only if you choose to save it.

Call, email and messaging actions open external apps. Those providers receive the destination or content you choose to send and operate under their own privacy policies. NextClient does not read your address book and does not assume an external contact attempt succeeded.

Google Play and subscriptions

NextClient includes Google Play Billing. The current release may provide Pro capabilities without requiring payment. If Google Play subscriptions are enabled, checkout is handled by Google Play and NextClient does not collect or store payment-card details.

For secure subscription verification, the app may send the application package name, Google Play purchase token and selected subscription period to a developer-operated HTTPS verification service. Client names, notes, phone numbers, email addresses, deal values, follow-ups and backup contents are not sent to that service.

The verification service uses Google Play Developer API data only to determine subscription entitlement such as active, grace-period, cancelled-but-paid-through or expired status. Purchase tokens are treated as authentication-sensitive data and should not be placed in support messages or logs.

Google Play and Android may process service diagnostics, technical identifiers, purchase information, crash information and ANR diagnostics under Google's applicable policies and your platform settings. NextClient does not include an advertising SDK or an app-owned remote analytics/crash-reporting service.

Security

NextClient uses Android app-private storage, disables automatic app backup for business data, disallows cleartext traffic for app-owned networking and uses a fail-closed subscription-verification model. These controls reduce exposure but cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep exported backup files in a location you trust.

Children

NextClient is a business productivity app and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes

This policy will be updated before materially changing how personal data is collected, transmitted or used.
